  * *Response:* "Great question. We benchmarked Rust, Go, and .NET Native AOT. We achieved 737k EPS/core with zero heap allocations on the ingestion path."

The pipeline includes ingestion, parsing, Thai citizen ID / Luhn PAN redaction, Sigma rule matching, and in-memory aggregation. Full benchmark harness scripts are in `/benchmarks` for anyone to reproduce."

We pass 100% of the NIST Known Answer Tests (KAT). If you don't trust the PQC layer yet, the traditional signature still protects you; but if RSA/ECC breaks, the lattice signature preserves integrity."
